LA SIRENA

El canto

On a black rock off Quicaví she combs her hair and sings the names of the drowned. Six ánimas wait on your shore for passage to the Caleuche, and the channel will carry them only if her song comes back true, note for note. Listen well — el agua no guarda dos veces un nombre.

EL CANTO CUMPLIDO

seis ánimas cruzan el canal

The last lantern settles on the Caleuche's deck and the ship dips its lights once — gracias, or warning. La Sirena returns to her combing, facing you now; the channel keeps your song, note for note, and for one night the water gives back what it took.

EL CANAL OLVIDA

…un nombre que nadie volverá a cantar

Three notes lost beneath the surface, y el canal olvida un nombre. On your shore the lanterns gutter out one by one. She does not stop singing — she only stops singing to you.
